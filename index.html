Our Story

Small studio, slow craft.

HYSS began at a single workbench with one idea: jewelry should feel personal, not precious. Every piece is designed in-house and finished by hand in small batches, so nothing is made that won't be loved.

We work only with recycled metals and responsibly sourced stones, and we'll repair or resize anything we make, for as long as you own it.

  • Recycled metals14k gold, gold vermeil & 925 silver
  • Hand finishedPolished and set in our studio
  • Lifetime careFree cleaning & repairs
